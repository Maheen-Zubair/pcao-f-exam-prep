## 🎯 27. Exam-Focused Vocabulary

| Term | Simple Meaning |
|---|---|
| **AI fluency** | Working with AI effectively, efficiently, ethically, and safely. |
| **4Ds** | Delegation, Description, Discernment, and Diligence. |
| **Automation** | AI performs a defined task. |
| **Augmentation** | Human + AI work together. |
| **Agency** | AI works toward a goal and chooses many steps. |
| **Delegation** | Decide what AI does vs. what humans do. |
| **Problem awareness** | Understand the goal, work, risks, and success criteria. |
| **Platform awareness** | Choose the right AI/tool for the task. |
| **Task delegation** | Assign work to a human or AI. |
| **Description** | Give AI the information and guidance it needs. |
| **Product description** | Describe what output you want. |
| **Process description** | Describe how AI should approach the task. |
| **Performance description** | Describe how AI should behave. |
| **Discernment** | Judge AI's output, justification, and behavior. |
| **Product discernment** | Judge the result/output. |
| **Process discernment** | Judge whether your AI workflow works effectively. |
| **Performance discernment** | Judge AI's independent behavior. |
| **Diligence** | Take responsibility for the AI-assisted work. |
| **Creation diligence** | Use responsible tools and data when creating. |
| **Transparency diligence** | Be honest about AI's role. |
| **Deployment diligence** | Check the result before using or releasing it. |
| **Context engineering** | Design all the information AI needs to perform the task well. |
| **Automation bias** | Trusting AI too easily. |
| **Hallucination** | Confident but incorrect or invented output. |
| **Redaction** | Remove identifying information while keeping useful patterns. |
| **Digital FTE** | An AI worker configured for a defined job. |
| **System of Record** | Trusted source of official business data and rules. |
| **System prompt** | Standing instruction that AI receives. |
| **Eval suite** | Repeatable tests used to evaluate AI output. |

# 🔑 Quick Revision

## 🧠 AI Fluency

- **AI fluency** → Work with AI effectively, efficiently, ethically, and safely.
- **4Ds** → **Delegation + Description + Discernment + Diligence**

### The 4Ds

| 4D | Remember It As |
|---|---|
| **Delegation** | Who does the work? |
| **Description** | What/how should AI do it? |
| **Discernment** | Is the AI doing it correctly? |
| **Diligence** | Am I taking responsibility? |

---

## 🤖 Levels of Human–AI Work

- **Automation** → AI performs a defined task.
- **Augmentation** → Human + AI work together.
- **Agency** → AI works toward a goal and chooses many steps.

**Easy distinction:**

> Automation = defined task  
> Augmentation = human + AI  
> Agency = AI chooses steps toward a goal

---

## 📋 Delegation

- **Problem awareness** → Understand the goal, work, risks, and success.
- **Platform awareness** → Choose the appropriate AI/tool.
- **Task delegation** → Assign the work to a human or AI.

---

## ✍️ Description

Three important types:

- **Product description** → **What** output do you want?
- **Process description** → **How** should AI approach it?
- **Performance description** → **How** should AI behave?

**Easy memory:**

> Product = WHAT  
> Process = HOW  
> Performance = BEHAVIOR

---

## 🔍 Discernment

Three important types:

- **Product discernment** → Is the **result** good/correct?
- **Process discernment** → Does the **workflow** work?
- **Performance discernment** → Is AI's **independent behavior** appropriate?

---

## 🛡️ Diligence

- **Creation diligence** → Use responsible tools and data.
- **Transparency diligence** → Be honest about AI's role.
- **Deployment diligence** → Check before using or releasing the output.

**Easy memory:**

> Creation → Responsible creation  
> Transparency → Be honest  
> Deployment → Check before release

---

## ⚙️ Important AI Concepts

- **Context engineering** → Give/design all the information AI needs.
- **Automation bias** → Trust AI too easily.
- **Hallucination** → AI produces confident but incorrect/invented information.
- **Redaction** → Remove identifying information while preserving useful patterns.
- **System prompt** → Standing instructions given to AI.
- **Eval suite** → Repeatable tests for checking AI outputs.

---

## 🏢 AI in Organizations

- **Digital FTE** → AI worker configured for a defined job.
- **System of Record** → Trusted source for official business data and rules.

---
